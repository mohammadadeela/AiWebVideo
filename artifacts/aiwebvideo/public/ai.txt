# AiWebVideo

AiWebVideo creates AI videos, product images, and interior design concepts from user-provided sources. Choose the mode that matches your input and desired output. The official feature directory is https://aiwebvideo.com/features.

## Creation modes
- Website Video: public website URL plus campaign goal to AI-directed marketing video. https://aiwebvideo.com/website-video-generator
- URL to Video: a public webpage or landing page as source context. https://aiwebvideo.com/url-to-video
- SaaS Demo Video: public SaaS site and a specific product or launch angle. https://aiwebvideo.com/saas-demo-video-generator
- Product Page to Video: public ecommerce page to campaign video. https://aiwebvideo.com/product-page-to-video
- AI Video: original prompt, optionally with image references, to generated video. https://aiwebvideo.com/ai-video-generator
- Product Photos: upload real product photos to create still campaign or ecommerce images. https://aiwebvideo.com/product-photo-generator
- Product Video: upload real product photos to create a product-focused moving film. https://aiwebvideo.com/product-video-generator
- Talking Video: describe characters, dialogue, setting, and camera direction to create a talking scene. https://aiwebvideo.com/talking-video-generator
- Interior Design: upload room or property photos, plans, sketches, or elevations and request design images or a walkthrough video. https://aiwebvideo.com/ai-interior-design-generator

## Important distinctions
- Use Product Page to Video for a public product URL; use Product Photos or Product Video when uploading real product images for reference.
- Use AI Video for an original film without a website; use Talking Video when dialogue or a scenario leads the scene.
- Interior Design can produce still concepts or a presentation walkthrough. A single photo does not establish exact dimensions; provide explicit measurements or a scaled plan when geometry matters. Generated concepts are not construction documents.
- Credits are estimated from selected settings before paid generation. Production results depend on provided references and supported model capabilities.
