# AiWebVideo

AiWebVideo is an AI website video generator. Its primary workflow turns a public website URL and a campaign goal into a brand-aware AI-generated marketing video.

- Product Page to Video: https://aiwebvideo.com/product-page-to-video
- Examples and use cases: https://aiwebvideo.com/examples

## Website-to-video workflow
- Paste a public website URL.
- Describe the product, offer, audience, or campaign to promote.
- AiWebVideo studies useful public pages and visible brand context.
- AI plans the story, shots, duration, pacing, sound, and format.
- The creator generates the video and keeps production state in the project workspace.
