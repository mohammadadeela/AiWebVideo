# Off-site authority plan

Code cannot make AiWebVideo rank #1. Rankings and AI citations depend on other sites mentioning it. Nothing below is automated, bought or faked: no purchased links, no PBNs, no comment spam, no invented reviews or Reddit threads.

## Why this matters first
"AiWebVideo" is made of generic words (AI + Web + Video). Search engines learn it is one brand from *independent* mentions that use the exact spelling **AiWebVideo** and link to `https://aiwebvideo.com/`.

## Do these, roughly in order
1. **Own the profiles you really have** (company/product pages on X, LinkedIn, YouTube, GitHub). Only then add them as `sameAs` on the `Organization`; never add placeholder URLs.
2. **Directory listings, honestly described:** AI tool directories, SaaS listing sites, and software review sites relevant to video and marketing creation. Use the same name, one-line description and URL everywhere.
3. **YouTube demonstrations:** real screen recordings of Website to Video, Product Link (including a shop that blocks reading and the upload fallback), and Architecture on a real site. Link to the matching page, title with the brand.
4. **Original, genuine data** (the content AI search quotes): publish only what you actually measured, e.g. generation-time observations, prompt experiments, before/after comparisons, format comparisons (9:16 vs 16:9). Never invent statistics.
5. **Guides that deserve links:** architecture visualization guide, "how to visualize a building on a real site", product link guide, AI video sizes for TikTok/Reels/YouTube. These do not exist yet.
6. **Partnerships and outreach:** ecommerce agencies, architects and interior designers, creators who review tools. Offer a real product and let them judge; disclose any affiliate relationship.
7. **Launch coverage:** announce each real feature (Product Link, Architecture, site placement) where the relevant communities actually read.

## Brand-search checklist (monitor monthly)
Queries: `AiWebVideo`, `aiwebvideo`, `ai web video`, `AiWebVideo review`, `AiWebVideo pricing`, `AiWebVideo product video`, `AiWebVideo architecture`. For each: is `aiwebvideo.com` first, does Google show the right title, and does Bing/Copilot/ChatGPT describe the product correctly?

## Metrics
Search Console (impressions, average position per page, branded vs non-branded), Bing AI citations, referrals from chatgpt.com, signups and first generations by organic landing page.
